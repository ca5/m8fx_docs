# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 16, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the **M8FX** application (hereby referred to as "Application") for iOS, developed by ca5.

## Data Collection and Use
The Application does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information, user data, telemetry, or analytics. We respect your privacy, and our Application operates entirely locally on your device.

## Microphone Access (Audio Input)
The Application requires access to your device's microphone (audio input) strictly for the purpose of receiving USB audio from connected external hardware (such as the Dirtywave M8 Tracker). 
- The audio data is processed locally on your device in real-time. 
- No audio recordings, streams, or related data are saved, transmitted, or shared with the developer or any third parties.

## Third-Party Services
The Application does not include any third-party analytics, tracking frameworks, or advertising services. 

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will notify you by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
